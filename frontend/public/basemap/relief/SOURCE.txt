Natural Earth I with Shaded Relief and Water, 1:50m (NE1_50M_SR_W)
Source: Natural Earth, https://www.naturalearthdata.com (public domain). Downloaded 2026-11-13 from
https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/50m/raster/NE1_50M_SR_W.zip

What it shows: a stylised, long-term picture of land cover (green = vegetated, tan/brown = dry or bare land,
white = ice) with shaded relief and water. It is NOT current vegetation, NOT fire or burn-scar data, and NOT a
satellite observation of any date.

Processing (scratch script relief_tiles.py, GDAL from the `astera` Conda env): reprojected from WGS84 to Web
Mercator (EPSG:3857, bilinear, full world extent, 8192 x 8192 px), polar rows outside the source filled with the
image's own ocean colour, cut into 512 px XYZ tiles for zoom 0-4 (box-average for lower zooms), JPEG quality 82.
Colours are unchanged; the app softens them per theme with MapLibre raster paint (see src/features/explorer/mapStyle.ts).
